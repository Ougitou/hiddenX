Scroll Trigger

Interactive Scroll-to-Audio Engine demo. Experience soundscapes generated exclusively as paragraphs scroll past the threshold line.

Engine Configuration & System Indexing Rules

  • Strict Target Selection: Only <p> elements are indexed. This container, headings, and lists are ignored.
  • Interaction Unlock: Modern browsers prevent autoplay. Scroll or click anywhere on the viewport to unlock the audio context.
  • Index mapping: The engine automatically detects the 0-indexed order of paragraphs.

Index 0
Paragraph One: The cold wind began to blow across the desolate, snowy plains. If you look closely at the debug overlay on your top-left, you will see Index 0 became active as this paragraph crossed the red threshold trigger line. A local wind ambient sound (ambient_wind.mp3) has been cued.

Index 1
Paragraph Two: This is the second paragraph in the system, mapped to Index 1. Because this paragraph does not have any data-audio attribute and isn't programmatically registered to any audio, it will scroll past the threshold line silently. It still gets highlighted and tracked in real-time.

Index 2 (OGG format)
Paragraph Three: Suddenly, footsteps crunching in the thick snow could be heard. This paragraph is mapped to Index 2 and plays footsteps.ogg. Notice how the previous playing audio immediately stops (or pauses) to ensure soundscapes never overlap messy or jarring sounds together.

Visual & structural break

This section is a div element containing text and headings. It does not get indexed as a paragraph. Notice that as this box scrolls past the trigger line, the active index remains on the previous paragraph, and no sound changes occur. Only real paragraph elements count!

Index 3
Paragraph Four: A heavy wooden door creaks open in the distance. This paragraph uses index 3 to play door_creak.mp3. You can configure options like viewportThreshold (where the trigger line sits), whether to stop previous audios, and replay settings.

Index 4
Paragraph Five: This is the final paragraph. It has Index 4. It does not play any audio, letting the story settle in quiet suspense. Scroll back up and down to experience the soundscapes triggering instantly and accurately.
